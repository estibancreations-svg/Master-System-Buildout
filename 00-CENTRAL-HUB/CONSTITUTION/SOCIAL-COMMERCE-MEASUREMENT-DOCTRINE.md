# Social Reach, Commerce & Forecasting Measurement Doctrine

**Status:** ACTIVE_CANON  
**Authority:** The Architect  
**Effective:** 2026-08-27

## Purpose

The enterprise must measure audience performance often enough to improve promotion, sales, forecasting, creative decisions, timing, and resource allocation.

This is a cross-system operating requirement, not a single social-media feature.

## Systems in Scope

At minimum:

- CMGIO
- Master Advertising Platform (MAP)
- AgencyFlow / Socials
- CEO Command Center
- Finance / Accounting surfaces
- Resource Intelligence
- THELMA forecasting and weekly intelligence
- Publishing & Media Studio where distribution metrics exist
- VisionWeaver where produced assets are published or promoted
- future commerce, CRM, campaign, creator, publishing, and audience systems

## Required Measurement Ledger

Where the platform/API makes the metric available, preserve:

- platform
- account/channel/page
- content/post/asset identifier
- campaign
- project/client
- publication date/time
- reporting period
- views
- impressions
- reach
- unique viewers where available
- watch time
- average view duration
- completion rate
- engagements
- reactions/likes
- comments
- shares
- saves
- clicks
- click-through rate
- followers/subscribers gained or lost
- leads
- conversions
- attributable orders/sales
- attributable revenue
- boost/ad spend
- cost per result
- CPM/CPC/CPA/ROAS where applicable
- organic vs paid designation
- source/API provenance
- retrieval timestamp
- attribution method
- confidence level

Unavailable metrics must be marked **UNAVAILABLE / NOT PROVIDED**. They must not be fabricated.

## Cadence

### Weekly

Generate a weekly operating report that includes:

- total views/reach and change from prior week;
- highest and lowest performing content;
- organic vs boosted performance;
- spend and attributable sales where evidence exists;
- engagement and conversion changes;
- platform/channel contribution;
- anomalies;
- emerging patterns;
- forecast update;
- recommended actions for creative, timing, audience, spend, boosting, repurposing, offers, and sales advancement.

This analysis becomes part of the enterprise weekly intelligence check.

### Monthly

At the close of every month, create and preserve a locked monthly snapshot containing:

- monthly totals;
- month-over-month change;
- weekly breakdowns;
- rolling averages;
- campaign/content rankings;
- paid vs organic contribution;
- attributable sales/revenue;
- boost/ad spend;
- cost efficiency;
- audience growth;
- forecast accuracy;
- major lessons;
- recommendations for the next month.

Monthly history must remain queryable for longitudinal analysis.

## Forecasting and Best-Practice Intelligence

THELMA and CMGIO should use the accumulated ledger to estimate and continuously recalibrate:

- likely view/reach ranges;
- likely engagement and conversion ranges;
- best posting windows;
- content formats with the strongest performance;
- audience segments;
- expected marginal return from boosting;
- sales impact;
- campaign saturation/fatigue;
- budget allocation;
- repurposing opportunities;
- production priorities.

Forecasts must show uncertainty/confidence and must be evaluated against actual outcomes.

## Accounting and Billing Connection

Social and advertising performance must connect to the financial layer where evidence permits:

`content/campaign -> spend -> views/reach -> clicks/leads -> conversions -> attributable sales/revenue -> margin/ROI`

Do not infer causation solely from correlation. Record the attribution model and confidence.

Where the work is client-billable, the ledger must support project/client attribution and billing evidence.

## Executive Reporting

The CEO Command Center should surface concise weekly/monthly indicators including:

- reach/views trend
- sales/revenue attributed to campaigns
- paid spend
- avoided/wasted spend where determinable
- strongest assets/channels
- underperforming assets/channels
- forecast
- recommended next actions

The Architect may drill from executive summary to source evidence.

## Governance

This doctrine is subordinate to the Base Ten Governance Standard. Automated recommendations are advisory unless explicitly delegated. The Architect retains final authority over boost, spend, campaign, creative, distribution, and sales strategy decisions.
