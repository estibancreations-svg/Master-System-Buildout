# THE ARC
## One-Page Teaser

**Format:** Premium one-hour science-fiction drama  
**Primary setting:** ARC-01 — Passover-Class Master Arc  
**Comparable positioning:** Large-scale prestige sci-fi with a grounded human ensemble, a repeatable world system, and a production-design bible built for long-form continuity.

## Logline
Inside a civilization-scale artificial world, an unscheduled systems activation exposes a hidden survival protocol and forces a systems ecologist, a ring mechanic, and a civic investigator to decide whether the Arc is malfunctioning, protecting them, or preparing for something outside.

## The Hook
The Arc is a ship, a city, a country, an ecosystem, and a machine. Its five branches carry homes, food systems, water systems, transit, civic power, hidden service decks, and under-panel oceans. When the ship begins transforming itself without authorization, every technical question becomes a political and moral one.

## Why Now
Audiences understand fragile systems: water, food, infrastructure, climate, governance, surveillance, automation, and institutional trust. The Arc turns those anxieties into a visually spectacular story engine.

## Season One
The first season begins with **The Passover**, a ceremonial transit through the central spine. Two shield panels move on their own. Green surfaces activate. Water begins flowing through approved top/bottom ring lanes. Officials call it a malfunction; workers call it old machinery; archived records call it something else.

## Core Characters
- **Mara Vale:** water-reclamation systems ecologist
- **Elias Ro:** senior ring mechanic
- **Liora Vale:** civic investigator
- **Talen Voss:** transit pilot
- **Nia Ro:** young witness to ecological changes
- **Soren Kael:** Core Authority administrator
- **The Custodian:** old operational intelligence / hidden protocol

## Franchise Potential
The Arc expands into multiple ship classes, books, graphic novels, art books, games, VR, collectibles, and a Digital Twin/VisionWeaver production system that keeps the world consistent across stories and media.

## Current Package
The project already includes a canonical visual base, proof-of-concept video tests, five ship-class prompt bibles, a 491-entity catalog, a 134-zone ARC-01 Digital Twin map, pitch deck, trailer plan, budget model, production plan, and IP/outreach workflow.
