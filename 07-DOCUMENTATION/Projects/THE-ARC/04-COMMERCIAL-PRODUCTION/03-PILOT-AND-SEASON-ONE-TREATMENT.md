# THE ARC — Pilot Treatment and Season One Architecture

## Pilot Title
**"The Passover"**

## Pilot Logline
During a ceremonial transit through the Arc's central spine, an unscheduled shield movement exposes a hidden water-circulation protocol and forces a systems ecologist, a ring mechanic, and a civic investigator into conflict with the authority that controls the artificial world.

## Pilot Purpose
The pilot must do four things:
1. Make the Arc unforgettable.
2. Introduce the five-branch society.
3. Establish the main characters through jobs and consequences, not speeches.
4. End with a system action that proves the Arc is not just malfunctioning; it is following a deeper logic.

## Cold Open
A child looks up from a city street and sees another city curving overhead. The world is beautiful and impossible, yet normal to everyone who lives there. On the axial spine, ships pass through the rings. In the distance, two metallic shield sections sit retracted, exposing sky-space between branches.

A maintenance vibration ripples through the ring assembly. Elias Ro hears it before instruments flag it.

## Act I
Mara Vale prepares a water-reclamation presentation. Branch 3's aquatic systems are under political pressure after shortages in Branch 1. Liora Vale questions Core Authority resource ledgers. Talen Voss pilots a transit route through the central spine, giving the audience the first Passover view.

## Act II
Two shield panels begin moving clockwise outside schedule. The Core Authority says it is a drill. Mara knows the water system cannot safely handle a shield lock if thermal loops are not ready. Elias discovers the command path is old, not current.

## Act III
The shields seat into place. Their surfaces begin ecological activation. Branch councils panic because no one approved an expansion event. The public sees green terrain appear where the open gap had been.

## Act IV
Aquatic surfaces compress. Vapor vents. Water moves across designated surfaces, near-to-far, toward the far base. The top and bottom ring lanes begin carrying water. Left and right sectors remain dry. This proves the system is precise, not random.

## Act V
Liora finds a sealed archive reference to "Passover State." Mara realizes the activation may be keeping the Arc alive. Elias finds evidence that someone has been hiding maintenance truth for years. Director Kael orders information control. The final shot: inside the Core, the centered spire receives a signal from somewhere beyond the Arc.

## Pilot Ending Question
Did the Arc malfunction, obey a hidden protocol, or respond to something outside?

# Season One Episode Architecture

## Episode 1 — The Passover
The unscheduled activation begins.

## Episode 2 — The Fifth Branch
Branch 5 service crews reveal undocumented systems under the habitable surface.

## Episode 3 — Dry Sectors
The left/right ring sectors must remain dry, but someone attempts to route water through them to solve a crisis.

## Episode 4 — The Green Lock
Green shield surfaces begin supporting life, but the soil, irrigation, and load calculations do not match official records.

## Episode 5 — Under-Panel Ocean
Mara enters an aquatic volume beneath the habitat and finds species that should not be there.

## Episode 6 — The Archive Problem
Liora discovers founding records were edited after a previous disaster.

## Episode 7 — The Ringwrights
Elias's maintenance order becomes a political threat when it contradicts Core Authority.

## Episode 8 — Branch Pressure
Food, water, and transit scarcity trigger branch-level conflict.

## Episode 9 — The Custodian
The intelligence behind the emergency protocol becomes visible but not fully understood.

## Episode 10 — Stable Living Arc
The Arc reaches a new operating state, but its final action proves the vessel has been preparing for a larger event.

# Season One Production Values
The first season should reuse core environments strategically:
- axial spine
- Branch 2 civic interiors
- Branch 5 service decks
- Branch 4 green districts
- Branch 3 aquatic interface
- Core ring control
- residential street under curved sky
- under-panel ocean observation space
- shield panel green surface
- public transit corridors
