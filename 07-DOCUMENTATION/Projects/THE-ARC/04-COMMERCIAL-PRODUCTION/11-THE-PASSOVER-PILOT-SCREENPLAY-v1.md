# TEASER

## 1. EXT. BRANCH ONE — MARKET STREET — ARTIFICIAL MORNING

A paper bird skims a canyon of towers.

It is not paper. It is a child's glider, no wider than a hand, folded from a translucent ration wrapper. It catches a warm current above a crowded street and climbs.

NIA RO, 15, quick-eyed and determined, follows it through pedestrians. Vendors uncover fruit. Tram cables wake with a low harmonic. Schoolchildren wear the same gray-green jacket in twenty personal variations.

Nia's glider rises past the roofline.

The sky is impossible.

Across a blue-lit atmospheric gulf, another city curves overhead — upside down from Nia's point of view, held to the inner world by rotation. Five immense habitat branches radiate around a protected central core. Between them: open gaps, distant stars, and retracted metallic shield surfaces waiting like unfinished pieces of a machine.

NIA
Come on. Come on.

The glider reaches the edge of the street's containment current.

It stops.

Not gradually. As if the air itself has made a decision.

The little craft rotates toward the central rings.

Nia watches it tremble.

A DROP OF WATER rises from a fountain beside her. Then another. For half a second, the droplets hang against gravity.

The city lights do not flicker. No alarm sounds.

The drops fall.

Her glider shoots toward the Core.

NIA
Hey—

A transit horn swallows her voice.

## 2. INT. RING SERVICE CRAWL — SAME

Dark. Tight. Metal sweating condensation.

ELIAS RO, late 40s, a senior ring mechanic whose body has learned every clearance the hard way, presses a listening tool against a structural rib.

Through it: the Arc's enormous body.

Fans. Pumps. Bearings. A million tons of life made audible.

Then a new sound.

Three low impacts separated by precise intervals.

THOOM.

Seven seconds.

THOOM.

Seven seconds.

THOOM.

Elias removes the tool.

His younger technician, JOREN, waits behind him.

JOREN
Bearing nine?

ELIAS
No.

JOREN
Stabilizer?

ELIAS
No.

JOREN
You going to tell me what it is?

ELIAS
It counted us.

The wall gives a faint answering TICK.

Elias looks toward the Core.

## 3. INT. AXIAL TRANSIT SHIP — CONTINUOUS

A passenger ship enters the central spine.

TALEN VOSS, 30s, transit pilot, composed because everyone is watching, guides it through the first ring. Beyond his glass: cities, parks, and water surfaces sweep around the rotating world.

Children crowd the observation blister.

TALEN

Laughter.

SEV

TALEN

SEV

TALEN

They pass beneath the centered spire.

For an instant every display shows the same word:

PASSOVER

Then normal navigation returns.

SEV

TALEN
I saw it.

A deep vibration crosses the ship.

Outside, one of the retracted shield panels MOVES.

A fraction. Clockwise.

No alarm.

CUT TO BLACK.
