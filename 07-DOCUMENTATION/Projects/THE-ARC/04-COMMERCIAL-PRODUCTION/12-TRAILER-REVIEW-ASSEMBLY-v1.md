## Mandatory visual QA

Reject or regenerate any shot that violates:

- exactly five habitat branches;
- squared branch ends;
- four stabilizing quadrants distinct from the branches;
- protected central ring geometry;
- centered antenna/spire;
- shield panels not counted as branches;
- no random or full-ring flooding;
- water only in designated aquatic zones;
- top/bottom ring lanes carry approved circulation;
- left/right ring sectors remain dry and transparent;
- no silent design drift from WORLD_01_MASTER / The Passover;
- no stock or watermarked reference material in the commercial cut.

- Branch 4 reads as agriculture, ecology and seed preservation.
