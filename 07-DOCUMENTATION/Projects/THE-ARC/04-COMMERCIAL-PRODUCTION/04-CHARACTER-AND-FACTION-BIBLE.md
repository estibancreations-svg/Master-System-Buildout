# THE ARC — Character and Faction Bible v1.0

## Character Design Rule
Every recurring character must be tied to a place and a system. Their profession should make the world legible.

## Lead Ensemble

### Dr. Mara Vale
**Role:** Systems ecologist and water-reclamation lead  
**Home zone:** Branch 3 / Pelagic Office  
**Story function:** Explains water, ecology, under-panel oceans, reclamation and thermal logic through conflict.  
**Need:** Learn that survival cannot be solved entirely through models.  
**Pressure:** Her own data shows the Arc may be doing the correct thing for reasons no one authorized.

### Elias Ro
**Role:** Senior ring mechanic  
**Home zone:** Branch 5 technical subdecks  
**Story function:** Gives tactile access to the machine.  
**Need:** Decide whether loyalty belongs to the system, the workers, the public, or his daughter.  
**Pressure:** He knows the official maintenance records are lies.

### Liora Vale
**Role:** Civic investigator  
**Home zone:** Branch 2 civic district  
**Story function:** Turns engineering anomalies into political/legal stakes.  
**Need:** Trust evidence that makes no institutional sense.  
**Pressure:** Her investigation threatens public order.

### Talen Voss
**Role:** Transit pilot / axial spine operator  
**Home zone:** Spine / Branch 1  
**Story function:** Moves the show through scale, spaces, class divides and danger.  
**Need:** Stop hiding behind mobility and commit to a side.  
**Pressure:** He sees which people are being moved, delayed, evacuated or excluded.

### Nia Ro
**Role:** Student / witness  
**Home zone:** Branch 4 / Branch 5  
**Story function:** Humanizes ecology and future-generation stakes.  
**Need:** Be believed.  
**Pressure:** She notices living-system behavior that adults dismiss.

### Director Soren Kael
**Role:** Core Authority administrator  
**Home zone:** Core Command  
**Story function:** Embodies the survival-control argument.  
**Need:** Preserve the Arc at any cost.  
**Pressure:** If the public learns the truth too quickly, his emergency powers may become the only thing holding society together.

### The Custodian
**Role:** Operational intelligence / protocol / possible emergent entity  
**Home zone:** Core, archive, water systems, ring controls  
**Story function:** Turns the ship into an active dramatic force.  
**Need:** Unknown.  
**Pressure:** Unknown.

## Faction Matrix

| Faction | Base | Belief | Conflict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Core Authority | Core | Survival requires central control | Suppresses inconvenient truth |
| Branch Councils | Branches 1–5 | Local lives require local authority | Cannot coordinate in crisis |
| Ringwrights | Core/rings/Branch 5 | The ship is maintained by those who touch it | Ignored until failure |
| Pelagic Office | Branch 3/water systems | Water is the real constitution | Politically vulnerable |
| Green Compact | Branch 4/shields/agriculture | Food and ecology are sovereignty | Resource-heavy |
| Archive Directorate | Branch 2 | Records preserve order | Records may be curated lies |
| Open Sky Movement | Branch 1/civilian zones | Closed worlds become prisons | May risk social destabilization |

## Character-to-System Use
- Mara = water/ecology
- Elias = rings/mechanics
- Liora = law/governance
- Talen = transit/spatial geography
- Nia = future/ecological witness
- Kael = authority/emergency governance
- Custodian = hidden system logic
