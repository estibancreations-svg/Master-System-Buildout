# THE ARC — 90-Second Proof-of-Concept Trailer Plan

## Trailer Goal
Make the viewer understand three things in 90 seconds:
1. The Arc is visually unforgettable.
2. The world has people, systems and rules.
3. Something inside the system has begun acting beyond normal control.

## Target Length
90 seconds.

## Structure

### 0:00–0:08 — Black / Sound Before Image
Low mechanical hum. Water in pipes. Distant city ambience. A child's voice: "You can see the sky, but it isn't above us."

### 0:08–0:20 — The Passover Reveal
Use the existing Passover-style interior flight. Slow axial movement. City surfaces curve overhead. Small craft pass the rings.

### 0:20–0:30 — People Live Here
Fast but elegant human-scale shots:
- Branch 1 street
- Branch 2 civic corridor
- Branch 4 green surface
- Branch 5 service deck
- Branch 3 aquatic observation window

### 0:30–0:42 — First Anomaly
Elias hears the rings. Warning lights remain calm. A shield panel begins moving. Someone says, "That wasn't scheduled."

### 0:42–0:55 — Systems Activate
Shield closure. Green lock. Vapor. Water begins moving across designated surfaces. Top/bottom ring lanes carry flow; left/right sectors stay dry.

### 0:55–1:05 — Political Pressure
Liora confronts Core Authority. Mara warns that water, heat and atmosphere are tied together. Kael orders containment.

### 1:05–1:17 — The Hidden World
Under-panel ocean. Marine life silhouettes. A child sees something in the water. Archive files open. The word PASSOVER appears as old protocol language.

### 1:17–1:25 — Escalation Montage
- transit surge
- ring passage
- emergency doors
- city looking upward
- spire signal
- Mara underwater-light reflection
- Elias inside a service corridor
- Liora in archive darkness

### 1:25–1:30 — Title
THE ARC  
"Some worlds are built to move. Some are built to remember."

## Voiceover Draft
"We thought the Arc was a ship. Then we thought it was a city. Then we learned it was a promise... and a warning."

## Sound Plan
- Layer 1: deep engine/substructure tone
- Layer 2: city ambience
- Layer 3: water and pressure systems
- Layer 4: ring movement metallic resonance
- Layer 5: restrained orchestral/electronic score
- Layer 6: final spire signal

## Required New Shots
1. Branch street with curved sky overhead
2. Branch 2 civic interior
3. Branch 5 service-deck interior
4. Branch 3 aquatic observation
5. Branch 4 agriculture/green surface
6. Core command room
7. Archive room
8. Under-panel ocean
9. Character closeups
10. Title-card shot

## Trailer QA
- Does it establish story, not just visuals?
- Does it preserve the canonical Arc?
- Does it show human stakes?
- Does it prove production potential?
- Does it leave a buyer wanting the pilot?
