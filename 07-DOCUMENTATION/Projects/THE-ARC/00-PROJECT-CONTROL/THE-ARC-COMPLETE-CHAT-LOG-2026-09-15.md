## 2.1 Inspiration and first world test

The session began with a discussion of the large artificial world shown in *Foundation* Season 3 Episode 5. The design was identified as comparable to an O’Neill-cylinder / rotating artificial-habitat concept. The project then moved into testing a similar civilization-scale artificial world in Runway.

The production strategy selected was **master image first, then animation**, to protect design continuity.

## 2.2 Canonical master world

The first successful canonical interior world image became:

- **Name:** `WORLD_01_MASTER`
- **Runway task:** `fcafcb09-7fd6-44cb-bdb7-1b753c72c422`
- **Visual identity:** colossal curved inhabited interior surfaces, cities, parks, waterways, central axial transport spine, repeating concentric rings, engineered ribs, open sky/space gaps and deep artificial-world horizon.

This image remains the primary production authority for ARC-01.

## 2.3 The Passover

The first successful 10-second 1080p animation used a slow stabilized forward flight parallel to the axial spine with a slight upward tilt, while preserving architecture, cities, core, rings and waterways. Subtle traffic/cloud/water movement was allowed; habitat rotation was nearly imperceptible and structural rings remained rigid.

- **Canonical video name:** `The Passover`
- **Runway task:** `0ed809d4-69e8-40ae-907d-4c87936566c7`

The user’s exact surviving reaction is archived above: **“Hot Shit!!! I like!”**

## 2.4 Canonical ARC-01 continuity locks

The following rules were progressively locked during the session and are now repository canon:

1. Exactly **five** major building-bearing habitat branches.
2. Branch ends are **squared/flat**, never rounded, bulbous or tapered.
3. Inter-branch spaces are gaps, not arms.
4. Four stabilizing structural quadrants remain distinct from the five branches.
5. The original central concentric ring system is protected: do not change ring count, spacing, proportion or silhouette.
6. Center antenna/spire remains aligned to the core axis.
7. Branch 2 design language is retained as a preferred massing/edge reference.
8. Two shield panels occupy inter-branch gap zones.
9. Shield motion is **clockwise**.
10. Retracted/moving shields read as metallic engineered surfaces.
11. Locked inward shield surfaces can become controlled green ecological terrain.
12. Aquatic surfaces compress/vent before controlled water deployment.
13. Water direction is near/foreground → far/base.
14. Water may flow through **top and bottom ring lanes only**.
15. Left/right ring sectors remain dry, empty and transparent.
16. Potable, weather/climate, agricultural/ecological, firefighting, thermal and open aquatic water are distinct functional subsets.
17. Aquatic ecosystems may support fish, dolphins, sharks, squid and other life where assigned.
18. Later centered/radial “Arc-around-an-Arc” imagery may be used as reference only; it does **not** replace ARC-01.
19. All future canonical ARC-01 production starts from `WORLD_01_MASTER` and/or `The Passover`.
20. No silent design drift.
