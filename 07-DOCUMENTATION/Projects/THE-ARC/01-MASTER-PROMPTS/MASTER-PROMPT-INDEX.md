# ARC STARSHIP MASTER PROMPT LIBRARY
## Design-Family Registry and Source Crosswalk

This package converts the distinct Arc starship/system designs created in the Runway development session into separate long-form master-prompt bibles. The later technical boards, branch studies, ecological states and water states are used as design evidence and subsystem references; they are not all falsely treated as separate ships.

## Registered Starship Design Families

### ARC-01 — PASSOVER-CLASS MASTER ARC
**Identity:** Canonical Production Base / Interior Artificial-World Starship  
**Role:** The original and controlling production design. All Master Arc animation and future canonical development begins from WORLD_01_MASTER and The Passover video.  
**Primary silhouette:** a colossal rotating artificial habitat whose inhabited surfaces curve around a vast central axial transportation spine, with city-bearing habitat sectors, kilometer-scale rings, ribs, sky apertures and a deep inhabited horizon  
**Hard continuity rule:** do not replace the canonical interior Arc with the later centered 'Arc-around-an-Arc' representation; do not change ring count/silhouette; do not add random oceans; do not round branch ends; do not lose the four quadrants

## Master Production Rule for the Canonical Arc

For the main Master Arc project, production and animation continue to originate from `WORLD_01_MASTER` and the first Passover video. The centered radial designs remain valid project-original starship options and reference systems, but they are not substituted as the canonical production base unless intentionally selected as a different ship.

## Shared Non-Negotiable Vocabulary
- Five building-bearing branches means exactly five.
- Inter-branch spaces are gaps, not arms.
- Branch distal ends are squared/flat, not rounded.
- Four stabilizing quadrants are structurally distinct from the five habitat branches.
- The central antenna/spire remains centered.
- Ring geometry must not drift between shots.
- Branch 2's approved outline remains a preferred habitat-edge reference.
- Aquatic, potable-water, weather and ecological systems are separate subsets and should not be visually collapsed into one undifferentiated water system.
- Water, when used in the established Arc circulation concept, moves near-to-far, returns toward the base, and uses top/bottom ring lanes while left/right ring sectors remain dry and transparent.
- Later centered/reference-board starship designs are cataloged as separate options rather than silently replacing the canonical Master Arc.
