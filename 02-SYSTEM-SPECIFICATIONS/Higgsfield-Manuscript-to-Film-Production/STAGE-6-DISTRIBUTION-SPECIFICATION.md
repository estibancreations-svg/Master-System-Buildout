# Stage 6 — Distribution Specification

**Stage ID:** HGFD-STAGE-6  
**Stage Name:** Distribution  
**Primary Outcome:** Governed publication, social, marketing, analytics, and commerce derivative package

---

## 1. Social Media Campaign Generation

Auto-generate short-form derivatives from the approved film, including:

- 15-second clips
- 30-second clips
- 60-second clips
- scene highlight reels

Each derivative must point back to the approved film version.

## 2. Platform-Specific Optimization

Prepare platform-specific outputs for TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn, and Twitter/X by varying:

- aspect ratio
- frame rate
- resolution
- caption style
- thumbnail/carousel format

## 3. Carousel Image Generation

Create one or more still images per approved scene for platforms such as Instagram and LinkedIn. Still derivatives must preserve source scene and clip references.

## 4. Caption and Hashtag Auto-Generation

Generated publication copy should include:

- caption draft
- hashtag pack
- CTA options
- platform safety notes

Human review remains required before posting unless explicitly authorized automation exists.

## 5. Multi-Platform Posting

Posting orchestration must support queueing, approval, retries, and destination receipts for:

- TikTok
- Instagram
- YouTube
- LinkedIn
- Twitter/X

## 6. Engagement Tracking and Analytics

Track:

- views
- watch time
- clicks
- saves / shares
- platform engagement
- conversion hooks where available

Analytics records must preserve platform source and collection timestamp.

## 7. Commerce Integration

Commerce derivatives may include product photography, promotional stills, or scene-based product callouts derived from approved assets.

## 8. Affiliate Link Tracking and Revenue Reporting

Every monetized distribution record should preserve:

- link identifier
- destination
- campaign mapping
- attributed revenue when available

## 9. Publication Preparation

Prepare outputs for Kindle and traditional publishing workflows where applicable, including:

- manuscript-derived publication package
- storyboard PDF export
- full production notes export
- rights / provenance support bundle

## 10. Sales and Readership Tracking

Publication follow-through should retain sales and readership metrics as downstream evidence linked back to the originating production package.

## 11. Final Distribution Gate

Distribution is complete only when destination packages, analytics hooks, publication exports, and provenance records have been captured truthfully.
